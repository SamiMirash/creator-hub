The rule

100% means everything.

Not trophies or achievements, the whole game. Every collectible and secret, and all endings, the way you would want it on a first playthrough.

1

Hidden by default.

Every guide opens with names and locations hidden. You reveal exactly as much as you want, one item at a time, so nothing is spoiled by accident.

2

Everything, in one run.

Built around 100% completion: all collectibles, hidden items, secret content, and every ending, tracked area by area so you never miss a thing.

3

Never lose a path.

When something ahead is permanent or branching, the guide tells you to back up your save first, so you can finish one path, reload, and still get the others in a single playthrough.
