NewsRPGExample placeholder2026-06-19

Big open-world RPG gets a major performance patch

The team behind one of last year's headline open-world RPGs has pushed a sizable update that mostly targets how the game runs. From what is being reported, frame pacing on consoles is noticeably smoother and a long-standing stutter when loading new areas has finally been addressed. There are also some quieter quality-of-life tweaks to the inventory and map screens. Nothing about the story changes, but for anyone who bounced off it because of the rough performance, it sounds like a good moment to jump back in.

NewsShooterExample placeholder2026-06-18

Long-awaited co-op shooter slips to early next year

A co-op shooter that a lot of people had pencilled in for this fall is now aiming for early next year instead. The studio framed the delay as wanting more time to polish the higher difficulty modes and stamp out matchmaking issues that showed up in testing. Disappointing if you were counting on it for the holidays, but a few extra months to land it properly is usually the better outcome. Expect a fresh gameplay look closer to the new window.

NewsIndieExample placeholder2026-06-17

Indie hit adds a free hard mode after community requests

One of this year's surprise indie successes just dropped a free update built almost entirely around player feedback. The headline addition is a tougher difficulty tier for people who felt the base game was too gentle once they learned the systems. There are also a handful of new accessibility options going the other direction, so the update widens the audience on both ends. It is a small team, and the fact that they keep shipping meaningful free content is doing a lot for their reputation.
