What this channel stands for

Sami Mirash welcomes everyone and treats everyone equally. This is an inclusive, tolerant, hate-free space. The whole idea is simple: I like everyone, we accept everyone, and we don't tolerate hate. Kindness, acceptance, inclusion, and human dignity for all people, regardless of who they are, where they come from, their religion, race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, or background.
