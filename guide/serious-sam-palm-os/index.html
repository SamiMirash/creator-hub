Hatshepsut

You are here when: The compact, grid-built Palm OS map identified as Hatshepsut in the level display.

This level contains 3 fixed sublevels; the enemy tally resets for each one.

Verify the Palm Controls and NETRICSA (missable)

Control setup

HintSpend a moment learning the hardware-button layout before committing to the maze.
WhereTest movement, turning, strafing, firing, weapon selection, and the information screen in the opening rooms.
Full detailUse Date Book and Address Book to turn; use the scroll control to move; hold Memo Pad while turning to strafe; fire with To-Do; tap the Graffiti area to cycle weapons; and press Find to open NETRICSA. Confirm each input works before advancing.
