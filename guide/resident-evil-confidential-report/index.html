Master Grid-Turn Movement

Core Mechanic

HintA harmless-looking turn can give every enemy another action.
WhereMovement is four-directional; stepping or changing facing advances the room's turn order.
Full detailPlan two moves ahead, face the intended firing lane before a target closes, use doors as reset points, and avoid exploratory spins in occupied rooms. If a fast target reaches the flank, retreat through the previous doorway instead of spending repeated turns rotating.

Use the Weapon Range Grid

Core Mechanic

HintA target must occupy a valid firing square.
WhereRaise the weapon and watch for a valid square within the coloured range pattern.
Full detailFace the lane, raise the weapon, and fire only when the target's square changes to the valid colour. Count handgun attacks as three rounds, then lower the weapon and reposition before another enemy action.
