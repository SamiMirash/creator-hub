Full detailFill the Fuel Canteen from a kerosene container, equip the Lighter, and use both beside a fallen body. A decapitating finish also prevents revival. Prioritize central corridors, where repeated travel makes an untreated body most costly.

Full detailLure two ordinary enemies into the same narrow corridor, defeat them on nearly the same patch of floor, fill the canteen, and ignite one body while both models overlap. Confirm both catch before leaving the camera angle.

Prepare Every Herb Mixture

WhereCollect green, red, and blue herbs and test every valid combination.
Full detailUse green alone, pair green with green, combine green with red, join green with blue, and make the three-color mixture. Blue removes poison; red must be combined and greatly strengthens a green herb.
