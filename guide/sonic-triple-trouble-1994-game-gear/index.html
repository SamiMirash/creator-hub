Sonic Route

Character route

HintOne character favours direct speed and rolling attacks.
WhereChoose Sonic at the character screen and begin a clean route.
Full detailKeep this state separate from Tails. Use Spin Dash for breakable floors and curves, Air Roll for airborne contact, and Super Peel Out when the forward lane is already safe.

Tails Route

Character route

HintThe second character can bypass selected vertical obstacles.
WhereChoose Tails at the character screen and begin a separate route.
Full detailUse the shared Spin Dash and Air Roll normally, but reserve limited flight for high ledges, tiny-platform crossings, and recovery from awkward launches. Land before the flight timer expires.

Spin Dash

Core move

HintBreakable terrain often yields to a charged start.
WhereHold Down, tap an action button, then release Down.
Full detailCharge while stationary, release toward open ground, and jump only after momentum is established. Use it for weak blocks and curved walls; never launch blindly toward pits or retracting spikes.

Air Roll

Core move

HintA second airborne input changes vulnerable contact.
WhereJump, then press an action button again while airborne.
Full detailThe brief rolling window lets you strike enemies or extend a rebound. Time the second press shortly before contact; it works once per bounce and ends quickly, so repeated button mashing is unreliable.

Super Peel Out

Character move

HintSonic has a standing acceleration option.
WhereHold Up, charge with an action button, then release Up.
Full detailUse it only on a visible straight. It produces immediate ground speed without Spin Dash protection, so cancel the idea when an enemy, wall, water edge, or blind drop sits ahead.

Tails Flight

Character move

HintShort vertical freedom opens safer alternate lines.
WhereJump as Tails, then press an action button repeatedly.
Full detailGain height with measured presses, steer toward the landing, and touch ground before trying again. Use flight to bypass tiny ledges or preserve fragile platforms, not to hover indefinitely.

Rocket Shoes

Gadget

HintA fast temporary gadget rewards a clear launch lane.
WhereBreak the Sonic-only rocket-footwear monitor in open terrain.
Full detailAim before collecting it because propulsion begins immediately. Steer through the intended high route, avoid fighting the forced momentum, and expect the gadget to end after its timed run or disruptive contact.

Propeller Shoes

Gadget

HintOne underwater tool replaces slow ordinary movement.
WhereFind Sonic's propeller-footwear monitor inside Tidal Plant.
Full detailEnter the water route first, collect the monitor, then steer through submerged passages while the effect lasts. It improves underwater travel but does not remove the need to watch air and collision hazards.

Jet Board

Gadget

HintSnowy slopes contain a dedicated travel device.
WhereCollect Sonic's board monitor near a winter slope.
Full detailTake the board only when the downhill lane is readable, centre it before jumps, and avoid hard obstacle contact. A hit ends the ride, so use ordinary movement for careful emerald-box work when necessary.

Pogo Spring

Gadget

HintA portable bounce tool reaches otherwise isolated ledges.
WhereBreak the Pogo Spring monitor before a high crossing.
Full detailControl horizontal movement during each bounce and land centrally on tiny platforms. Sonic needs this for one emerald-box route; Tails can often fly, but should still demonstrate the gadget once.

Sea Fox

Gadget

HintTails receives a vehicle for submerged combat.
WhereCollect the Tails-only submarine monitor in Tidal Plant.
Full detailStay near the upper-left during the submerged encounter and fire forward. Your shots damage the target and can intercept higher projectiles; direct contact can knock Tails out of the vehicle.

Ring Protection

Survival rule

HintDamage does not empty the counter uniformly.
WhereCarry rings and note the thirty- or fifty-ring loss after contact.
Full detailOrdinary damage removes a fixed block rather than every ring, but pits, drowning, crushing, and timeout remain lethal. Preserve at least fifty before an emerald box and one during extended mechanical encounters.

Hundred-Ring Life

Resource milestone

HintA large ring reserve has a direct reward.
WhereRaise the counter to 100 during any normal act.
Full detailCollect the hundredth ring without dying or spending the route on a one-use box first. The extra life is awarded immediately; later rings begin building the next hundred normally.

Turnstile Bonus Set

Goal bonus

HintThe act exit can display several outcomes.
WhereCross Act 1 or Act 2 turnstiles and watch the symbol.
Full detailRecord the ring award and both character-life awards as they appear across replays. Some symbols give no resource. Do not mistake the randomized panel for an ending requirement or emerald state.

Emerald Box Rule

Special-stage entry

HintThe hidden box only works with a prepared ring count.
WhereReach an emerald box with at least fifty rings.
Full detailDo not break the box below fifty: each placement works once and becomes a Super Ring afterward. With fifty or more, break it and touch the star circle above it to enter the current uncleared Special Stage.

Hidden mode

HintThe title prompt has an alternate audio menu.
WhereAt the title screen, use the directional input until Sound Test appears.
Full detailEnter the menu and play every selectable number long enough to distinguish it, recording both ends of the range. Return with Start; do not treat compilation-specific controls as cartridge controls.

HintA standalone course is hidden beside the audio menu.
WhereSelect Time Attack from the alternate title prompt, then choose Sonic.
Full detailFinish the unique mixed-theme course from its normal start and allow the result to return to the title. Record the completed time; level select does not substitute for this dedicated mode.

Time Attack — Tails
