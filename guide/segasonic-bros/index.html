Loop-Clearing Rules

Learn the enclosure system before committing to the Level 99 run.

Read the Next Group

Puzzle skill

HintPlan one arrival ahead instead of reacting at the floor.
WhereUse the center preview to inspect the next two-by-two color group.
Full detailBefore the active group settles, read all four preview colors in the center column. Choose a landing that helps one color close an enclosure while leaving the other colors reachable.

Steer a Falling Group

Puzzle skill

HintPlace the whole formation before worrying about a clear.
WhereUse left and right to align the two-by-two group over the chosen columns.
Full detailMove the falling group horizontally while it still has clearance. Avoid parking against a wall until its orientation is correct, because a two-column piece has little recovery room near a tall stack.

Rotate a Falling Group

Puzzle skill

HintThe four colors can be rearranged before landing.
WherePress Button 1 while the two-by-two group is falling.
Full detailTap Button 1 to rotate the four Sonic pieces, then recheck which two colors will touch the floor or stack. Rotate high on the board; late turns beside a wall may have no valid space.

Accelerate a Safe Drop

Puzzle skill

HintBank time only after the landing is settled in your head.
WhereHold Down after the group is aligned and oriented.
Full detailMove and rotate first, then hold Down to speed the descent. Release it if the landing preview exposes an unwanted isolated color; speed-dropping an unplanned group is the quickest way to seal a useful channel.

Close a First Enclosure

Puzzle skill

HintMatching here is about surrounding space, not making a straight row.
WhereConnect one color around a pocket of pieces until its outline closes.
Full detailBuild a continuous boundary from one color around any enclosed pieces. When the final same-colored connection lands, the boundary and everything inside it disappear; a glowing outline signals a closure the game will accept.

Use the Floor as a Boundary

Puzzle skill

HintA loop does not need four handmade sides.
WhereBuild a same-colored arch whose open edge touches the bottom of the grid.
Full detailRun one color down both sides of a pocket and reconnect it above while the pocket rests on the floor. The bottom boundary completes the enclosure, clearing the arch and its contents without a matching row beneath it.

Use a Side Wall as a Boundary

Puzzle skill

HintThe outer columns can close a shape for you.
WhereBuild a same-colored hook against either vertical edge.
Full detailTouch one side wall with both ends of a same-colored enclosure and connect the remaining path inside the grid. The wall counts as the missing boundary, letting a half-loop clear safely along the edge.

Clear a Large Enclosure

Scoring skill

HintThe space inside a loop matters as much as its border.
WhereKeep extending a safe boundary until it surrounds several mixed pieces.
Full detailPrepare a broad floor- or wall-assisted enclosure, confirm the glowing path, and close it only when several pieces sit inside. The entire captured region disappears together and produces a much larger score gain than a tiny pocket.

Preserve a Low Emerald Channel

Puzzle skill

HintA narrow path to the floor becomes valuable at each decade.
WhereKeep at least one column low as the level counter approaches a multiple of ten.
Full detailRoute ordinary groups away from one open column before Levels 10, 20, and every later tenth level. The incoming emerald removes its landing row and everything above it, so a floor-level landing can empty the whole board.

Avoid a Blocked Entry

Failure warning

HintThe attempt ends when a new group cannot descend.
WhereWatch the highest occupied cells beneath the spawn area.
Full detailDo not build directly under the entry columns unless the next move clears space. If an arriving group has no legal descent, the run ends and the continue countdown begins; reload the last clean decade state for a mastery attempt.
