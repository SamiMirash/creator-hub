Rank Factor

HintAccurate weak-point shots add a separate score component.
WhereDivide the critical-hit percentage among defeated enemies by three.
Full detailRecord both defeated targets and confirmed critical defeats, then use the result-screen percentage divided by three. A missed shot does not directly reduce this component unless it changes the displayed ratio.

Clear-Time Contribution

Rank Factor

HintFaster completion provides up to thirty points.
WhereEarn 30/25/20/15/10/5 points below 120/135/150/180/240/300 minutes.
Full detailCompare the final clear time against the first strict boundary it satisfies: under 120 minutes gives 30; then under 135 gives 25, under 150 gives 20, under 180 gives 15, under 240 gives 10, and under 300 gives 5.

Save Contribution

Rank Factor

HintFewer typewriter uses preserve up to fifteen points.
WhereEarn 15 points with zero saves, 10 below four, or 5 below eight.
Full detailFor a maximum attempt, make no in-game save and rely on a separate cloned starting file for practice. Otherwise finish with one to three saves for 10 points, or four to seven for 5; eight or more earns none here.

Spray Contribution

Rank Factor

HintFull-recovery sprays reduce one scoring category.
WhereEarn 15 points with none, 10 below two, or 5 below four sprays.
Full detailUse herbs or Herbal Medicine when recovery is necessary. Zero Medicinal Sprays gives 15 points; exactly one gives 10; two or three give 5; four or more provide no points in this factor.

Top Agent Bonus

Rank Bonus

HintA low-combat, no-assistance route grants the largest bonus.
WhereFinish below 30 defeated enemies with zero saves and zero Medicinal Sprays.
Full detailPlan a bypass route, defeat only mandatory targets, and confirm the final defeated count remains under 30. Do not save or use Medicinal Spray at any point; meeting all three conditions adds 50 points.

Sniper Bonus

Rank Bonus

HintA combat-heavy precision route earns a different bonus.
WhereDefeat more than 100 enemies while keeping critical hits above 75 percent.
Full detailUse silenced or standard handguns for controlled weak-point shots, avoid wasteful body finishes, and verify both final values: defeated count must exceed 100 and the critical-hit percentage must exceed 75. The bonus adds 40 points.

Ninja Bonus

Rank Bonus
