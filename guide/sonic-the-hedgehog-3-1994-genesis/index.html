Tails Alone Route

Character route

HintAerial movement reveals a distinct set of vertical routes.
WhereChoose Tails alone on the remaining save slot.
Full detailComplete all acts as Tails alone. Tap the jump button again to fly, refresh before his tails tire, and use swimming underwater. He may collect the full emerald set, but this cartridge gives him no transformation.

Insta-Shield

Movement technique

HintSonic has a brief aerial technique that safely extends an attack.
WhereWhile airborne without an elemental shield, press a jump button again.
Full detailJump as Sonic, then tap A, B, or C just before contact. The flash briefly enlarges his attack boundary; use it to hit small openings while keeping his body clear of spikes, arms, or drill tips.

Flame Shield

Elemental shield

HintOne shield turns fire hazards into safe approach windows.
WhereBreak the red elemental monitor and test a second jump in open air.
Full detailThe Flame Shield blocks fire and converts Sonic's airborne second press into a horizontal burst. Water removes it immediately, so spend its mobility before entering a pool.

Water Shield

Elemental shield

HintThe blue shield changes both breathing and downward movement.
WhereBreak its monitor, enter water, then press jump again on land.
Full detailThe Water Shield removes the drowning timer. On land, Sonic's second jump input drives him straight down and rebounds from the floor; use repeated bounces to attack or reach vertical openings.

Lightning Shield

Elemental shield

HintA magnetic shield improves both collection and jump height.
WhereBreak the yellow elemental monitor and jump twice near loose rings.
Full detailThe Lightning Shield pulls nearby rings toward the character. Sonic gains a true double jump while wearing it, but touching water removes the shield, so collect high passages first.

WhereCross an act goal when the timer reads precisely 9:59.
Full detailUse a cleared-file act select, wait near the goal with a safe ring, and trigger completion during 9:59 before the ten-minute timeout. The tally awards 100,000 points; attempt this on a disposable run because one second late costs a life.
