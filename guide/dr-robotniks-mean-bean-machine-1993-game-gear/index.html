Make a Four-Bean Clear

Board rule

HintStart with the game's fundamental colour rule.
WhereJoin four matching beans through horizontal or vertical contact.
Full detailBuild any connected group of four or more identical colours. Corners alone do not connect, so confirm that every member shares an edge before locking the trigger pair.

Use an Uneven Landing

Board rule

HintA horizontal pair can separate across different heights.
WhereLay a pair across columns whose stacks end at different rows.
Full detailRotate the pair horizontally before it lands. The supported half stops first while the other continues downward, letting one drop place two colours at separate heights.

Remove Refugee Beans

Board rule

HintNeutral blockers respond to a nearby colour clear.
WhereClear a coloured group sharing an edge with at least one Refugee Bean.
Full detailDo not try to match Refugees together. Form a normal four-colour group beside them; every neutral blocker touching that clearing group disappears during the same resolution.

Create a Chain Reaction

Board rule

HintPrepare one clear to drop another into place.
WhereArrange two colour groups so the first removal completes the second.
Full detailBuild the lower trigger without prematurely completing the upper colour. Clear the first group, let gravity join the next four, and wait for every link to resolve before placing again.

WhereCheck the small preview beside the board before committing the current drop.
Full detailChoose a safe landing for the active pair while accounting for both colours in the preview. Preserve at least one flexible lane when neither upcoming colour helps your current chain.
