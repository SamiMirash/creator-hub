Control technique

Control technique

WherePress Start during a board to pause, then press it again to resume.
Full detailUse Start only after the falling pair is positioned. Resume deliberately so an accelerated or poorly aligned pair does not lock unexpectedly.

Clear an Orthogonal Group

Core mechanic

HintBuild a compact group without using diagonals.
WhereJoin at least four same-colour beans through shared sides.
Full detailMake a vertical, horizontal, L-shaped, T-shaped, or clustered group of four or more. Corner-only contact does not connect the group and will not clear.

Split a Pair on Uneven Ground

Core mechanic

HintA horizontal landing can separate its two halves.
WherePlace a horizontal pair across columns with different stack heights.
Full detailLand one half on the taller column; the unsupported bean drops to the lower available cell. Use this to feed a buried colour without blocking its neighbour.

Create a Falling Chain

Advanced mechanic

HintArrange one clear to complete another below.
WhereRemove a supporting group so the beans above fall into a second match.
Full detailBuild the later match first, separate one required colour with a supporting group, then clear that support. The fall completes the next group and counts as another chain link.

Remove Refugee Beans

Defence mechanic

HintClear colour beside the unmatchable pieces.
WhereMake a normal four-bean clear directly adjacent to Refugee Beans.
Full detailRefugee Beans cannot match with one another. Clear any qualifying coloured group touching them by an edge; every adjacent Refugee disappears with that clear.

Use the Next-Pair Meter

Planning mechanic

HintPlan one placement beyond the active pair.
WhereRead the paired colours displayed between the two boards.
Full detailBefore locking the current pair, reserve a column or support for the previewed colours. Recheck the meter after each lock because it advances immediately.

Confirm the Loss Column (missable)

Board boundary

HintThe whole top edge is not equally dangerous.
WhereObserve the third column from the left at the board's ceiling.
Full detailUse a disposable Exercise state and fill that column until a piece occupies its top entry cell. The board ends even if neighbouring columns still have open space.
