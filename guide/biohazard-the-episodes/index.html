Setup

HintCombat changes the meaning of the direction pad.

Full detailEnter a repeatable early encounter, hold the aim soft key, use Up and Down to alter elevation, then use Left and Right to cycle visible targets. Fire once with Confirm and verify releasing aim restores ordinary movement.
