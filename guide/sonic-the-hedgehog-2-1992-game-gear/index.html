Cartridge Completion Rules

You are here when: The handheld title screen, act entrances, ring counter, and spinning goal panels.

This cartridge has no native save; preserve act entrances before testing finite rewards.

Earn the 100-Ring Life (missable)

Completion task

HintA full ring counter carries a permanent reward.
WhereCollect 100 rings within one life before an act ends.
Full detailUse a ring-rich Act 1 or Act 2, avoid damage, and collect the hundredth ring. The counter rolls over and immediately adds one life; confirm the life total before continuing.

Trigger the Ring Goal Panel (missable)

Hidden reward

HintThe panel notices tidy ring totals.
WhereCross an Act 1 or Act 2 panel with zero rings or a multiple of ten.
Full detailApproach the panel carrying 0, 10, 20, 30, or another exact multiple of ten, without satisfying a higher-priority condition. Cross it once and let the panel settle; the ring face adds ten rings.

Trigger the Sonic Goal Panel (missable)

Hidden reward

HintA rough act can return one lost chance.
WhereLose two lives, then finish an Act 1 or Act 2.
Full detailStart from a disposable entrance state, deliberately lose two lives, and clear the act on the next attempt. Cross the panel with a non-special ring total; the Sonic face restores one life. Reload afterward if conserving lives.

Trigger the Tails Goal Panel (missable)

Hidden reward

HintOne exact ring total earns insurance.
WhereReach an Act 1 or Act 2 panel carrying exactly 77 rings.
Full detailCount carefully and stop collecting at 77. Protect that total to the panel, cross it, and wait for the Tails face; it awards a Continue that can rescue the run after the final life is lost.

Record the Default Goal Panel (missable)

Hidden result

HintThe ordinary result appears when no reward rule applies.
WhereFinish an Act 1 or Act 2 with a ring total outside every listed condition.
Full detailUse a safe total such as 31 while avoiding the two-life-loss rule, then cross the panel. Let the default face appear and confirm that no bonus is added; this completes the four-result panel record.

Use a Continue (missable)

Completion task

HintTest the run insurance after earning it.
WhereAfter obtaining a Continue, exhaust the current life stock once.
Full detailPreserve a state, earn the Tails panel reward, then intentionally use every remaining life. Choose to continue when prompted and verify that play resumes; reload the preservation state afterward.

Open the Level Select

Hidden mode

HintThe title animation hides a precisely timed input.
WhereHold down-left and both action buttons from boot, then press Start on the third eye blink.
Full detailFrom the SEGA logo onward, keep down-left plus buttons 1 and 2 held. Skip the opening with Start but keep holding. At the title, watch the small companion figure: press Start again when its eye fully closes on the third blink. A ring sound confirms the stage menu; retry from a hard-reset state if ordinary play begins.

Record the Extended Opening (missable)

Version-specific presentation

HintDo not skip the boot sequence on the first run.
WhereLet the Game Gear opening play completely from the SEGA logo to the title screen.
Full detailHard-reset from the clean boot state and make no input. Record the entire darker-sky opening until control reaches the title screen; it contains additional animation absent from the Master System build. Use the separate title state afterward so this record cannot be lost.

Record the Game Gear Music Variants (missable)

Version-specific presentation

HintSeveral transition cues differ on this cartridge.
WherePreserve clean audio from one Act 3 encounter and both completion-result states.
Full detailRecord an Act 3 encounter with effects low enough to identify its theme. Later, preserve the alternate completion result and the full-route result from separate states; each uses Game Gear-specific music. Label the recordings by physical route only, without adding narrative descriptions.
