Cabinet Setup and Controls

You are here when: The MAME launch screen and the cabinet-input assignment menu before a paid cycle begins.

Use the preserved arcade ROM set; this driver is fully playable but does not support emulator save states.

Verify the Arcade Build

System task

HintConfirm the archive before changing controls or cabinet switches.
WhereLaunch MAME shortname soniccar from soniccar.zip and run the emulator's ROM verification.
Full detailUse the installed 359,312-byte archive. Its ZIP SHA-1 is c1914d2a849cae10f8d195986fe14bb89f098264, and MAME 0.289 must report the soniccar ROM set as good before play.

Map Steering Left and Right

Control function

HintSeparate road steering from the other left-right control pair.
WhereMap P1 Right Stick Left and Right to the wheel directions.
Full detailOpen Input Assignments for this machine. Bind P1 Right Stick/Left and P1 Right Stick/Right to comfortable directions, enter a cycle, and confirm the patrol car crosses the lane in both directions.

Map Both Winker Directions

Control function

HintThe cabinet has a second two-way lever that does not steer.
WhereMap P1 Left Stick Left and Right to the two indicator directions.
Full detailBind both P1 Left Stick directions separately from steering. During the road section, tap each once and confirm its corresponding indicator response; this function is cosmetic and should not move the car.

Map the Siren and Lights

Control function

HintOne dashboard control operates the emergency-light response.
WhereMap P1 Button 1 to the siren control.
Full detailBind P1 Button 1, begin a cycle, and press it after the vehicle is active. Confirm both the siren response and the cabinet-light animation; it does not replace steering or acceleration.

Map Spin Jump

Control function

HintThe action button becomes important only late in the route.
WhereMap P1 Button 2 to Spin Jump.
Full detailBind P1 Button 2 and test it during the road section. The vehicle continues forward while the rider leaves it briefly; retain this binding for the close-range pursuit interaction.

Map Acceleration

Control function

HintKeep ordinary forward speed on its own input.
WhereMap P1 Button 3 to acceleration.
Full detailBind P1 Button 3 and hold it on a clear straight. Confirm the forward pace rises without activating the separate turbo effect, then release it before testing the stronger speed input.

Map Turbo

Control function

HintReserve one control for the stronger overtaking burst.
WhereMap P1 Button 4 to turbo.
Full detailBind P1 Button 4. On an unobstructed lane, hold acceleration, add turbo briefly, and verify the stronger exhaust and closing speed before releasing it near traffic.

Operator Options

You are here when: The Machine Configuration menu containing the SW2 cabinet switches.

These switches reproduce cabinet presentation options; they do not add a second gameplay route.

Check Every Demo-Sound Interval

Cabinet option

HintThe attract sequence can announce itself at several intervals.
WhereUnder Machine Configuration, inspect Demo Sound Interval.
Full detailCycle through Off, Every 4 Minutes, Every 2 Minutes, and On. Return it to On for ordinary archival capture so each attract cycle retains its expected sound.

Check Both Lighting-Time Modes

Cabinet option

HintCabinet lights can operate during different portions of the cycle.
WhereInspect Lighting Time under Machine Configuration.
Full detailTest Advertise & Playtime and Playtime Only. The setting governs when the external cabinet-light output is active; use Advertise & Playtime when documenting the complete attraction behavior.

Check the Cabinet-Light Toggle

Cabinet option

HintOne switch globally enables the external lighting output.
WhereInspect the Light switch under Machine Configuration.
Full detailToggle Light Off and On while observing the emulated output state. Leave it On for the completion run, because Off suppresses a real cabinet function rather than changing the road sequence.

Check Both Payment Messages

Cabinet option

HintThe payment prompt has a region-neutral wording alternative.
WhereInspect Screen Display under Machine Configuration.
Full detailSwitch between Insert 100–400 Yen and Insert Money, confirm both attract-screen messages, then retain the original yen wording for the Japanese cabinet route.

City Road

You are here when: The vertically scrolling three-lane street bordered by bright pavements and planted verges.

Traffic advances automatically; the route cannot be reversed after an obstacle scrolls away.

Start a Paid Cycle (missable)

Route task

HintBegin from the quiet attract display with every control already mapped.
WhereInsert one coin and wait for the vehicle-departure sequence.
Full detailWith the original payment wording selected, insert Coin 1 once. Do not add further credits or reset; let the departure and control prompt finish until the vertically scrolling road accepts steering.

Complete the Road-Control Prompt (missable)

Route task

HintUse the opening clear street to test movement safely.
WhereAt the first steering prompt, move left and right without touching the roadside.
Full detailCentre the vehicle, steer once toward each side, then return to the middle lane before traffic reaches the screen. Demonstrate siren, both winker directions, acceleration, turbo, and one jump while space remains.

Perform a Clean Overtake (missable)

Driving action

HintWait for a straight gap before using the strongest speed control.
WhereMove into an empty lane, hold acceleration, and add turbo to pass one civilian vehicle without contact.
Full detailRead the lane ahead before moving. Shift fully into the open lane, straighten the wheel, hold acceleration, tap turbo until the slower vehicle falls behind, and release turbo before the next traffic group.

Record One Collision Spin (missable)

Driving action

HintA separate low-rank run should document the road's penalty response.
WhereTouch a civilian vehicle or the roadside once and observe the forced spin.
Full detailUse a run reserved for the lower result grades. Steer into one traffic car at low relative speed, release turbo during the spin, regain the centre lane, and note that every repeated impact lowers the eventual star result.

Clear Traffic Without a Collision (missable)

Driving challenge

HintPrioritise open road over constant maximum speed.
WhereFrom the control prompt to the pursuit transition, avoid every vehicle, kerb, and roadside boundary.
Full detailStay with left-side traffic flow, scan one screen ahead, and change lanes early. Use ordinary acceleration through mixed traffic; reserve turbo for fully visible gaps and release it before intersections or side-by-side cars.

Reach the Pursuit Cleanly (missable)

Route task

HintKeep the final road approach centred and leave room ahead.
WhereEnter the final straight without colliding during the last traffic group.
Full detailAfter the dense road group, return to the centre lane and hold ordinary acceleration. Do not chase a disappearing civilian car into the verge; the red target vehicle arrives automatically when the pursuit begins.

Final Pursuit

You are here when: The long straight road with a single red vehicle travelling ahead of the patrol car.

The route finishes even if the target vehicle remains intact; its destruction is a separate completion action.

Close the Pursuit Gap (missable)

Pursuit action

HintUse speed in short bursts while preserving lateral control.
WhereHold acceleration and apply turbo on the clear straight until the red vehicle is within jump range.
Full detailApproach from directly behind but leave roughly one vehicle length. Release turbo before steering around an incoming hazard, re-centre, then rebuild the gap rather than colliding with the target's rear bumper.

Land a Spin-Jump Attack (missable)

Pursuit action

HintWait until the target fills the space immediately ahead.
WhereAt close range behind the red vehicle, press the mapped jump button and make contact from above.
Full detailAlign the patrol car with the target's centre, close to about one car length, release turbo, and press Jump. A successful hit shows the airborne rider contacting the red vehicle instead of landing on empty road.

Destroy the Red Target Vehicle (missable)

Pursuit challenge

HintRepeat accurate jumps without sacrificing lane control.
WhereContinue landing close-range jump attacks until the red vehicle breaks apart before the pursuit ends.
Full detailAfter each confirmed hit, re-enter the patrol car, re-centre, and rebuild a safe one-car gap. Dodge lateral hazards with steering, then repeat the square approach and jump until the destruction animation appears.

Finish with the Target Vehicle Intact (missable)

Outcome variant

HintOne replay should preserve the pursuit's optional non-destruction outcome.
WhereReach the pursuit, avoid all jump attacks, and let the final straight end with the red vehicle intact.
Full detailUse a dedicated cycle. Follow at a safe distance, steer around hazards, and never press Jump near the red vehicle. The ride still advances to its return and result sequence, proving destruction is optional.

Complete the Full Route (missable)

Route task

HintStay with the automatic return sequence after the pursuit closes.
WhereFinish the pursuit and remain through the return drive until the result emblem appears.
Full detailDo not reset when the straight road clears. Let the automatic return to the starting building finish, wait through the transition, and record the complete star emblem before attract mode resumes.

Result Ranks

You are here when: The blue patterned result screen with a large badge and one to five visible stars.

The star screen is the cycle's performance result. The five-star animation contains rapid flashing.

Record the One-Star Result (missable)

Result variant

HintUse one deliberately poor driving cycle for the lowest badge.
WhereAccumulate more than forty collision spins before completing the route.
Full detailChoose a low-rank replay, repeatedly steer into traffic or the boundary, and allow every spin to resolve before the next impact. More than forty crashes is the verified practical target; finish the route and capture the one-star badge.

Record the Two-Star Result (missable)

Result variant

HintPlan a heavily penalised run, but stop well before the lowest-rank total.
WhereComplete the route after roughly twenty to twenty-five collision spins.
Full detailCount resolved spins rather than brief scrapes. Aim for 20–25 deliberate collisions, then drive normally through the remaining road and pursuit. Record the two-star badge; if it drops to one, reduce the next run's impacts.

Record the Three-Star Result (missable)

Result variant

HintUse a middling run between heavy crashing and careful driving.
WhereComplete the route with intermittent collisions until the result settles at three stars.
Full detailThe game does not display an exact threshold. A locally verified neutral autoplay with no steering and repeated road contact earned three stars. If you receive two, reduce impacts; if you receive four, add several controlled spins next cycle.

Record the Four-Star Result (missable)

Result variant

HintDrive cleanly overall while retaining a small, deliberate penalty.
WhereAllow only a few controlled collisions, then finish the pursuit successfully.
Full detailStart from a clean-run plan, permit one resolved collision, and finish without further contact. If the result remains five stars, add one collision next cycle; if it falls to three, remove impacts until the four-star badge appears.

Record the Five-Star Result (missable)

Result variant

HintTreat every road contact as a restart condition.
WhereComplete the route without a collision and destroy the red target vehicle before the pursuit closes.
Full detailDrive each traffic group at controllable speed, use turbo only through empty lanes, and restart after any forced spin. In the pursuit, align directly behind the target and land repeated jumps until it breaks apart. Capture the five-star badge; expect rapid flashing.
