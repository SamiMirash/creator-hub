LAN Multiplayer Modes

You are here when: The Windows multiplayer menu reached from the title screen through LAN Game.

Official internet matchmaking is unavailable; use two Windows PCs on a LAN or a private virtual LAN for the stock modes.

Win a Deathmatch round

Game mode

HintTry the free-for-all rules in a private LAN session.
WhereHost Deathmatch for at least two players and reach a finite score or time limit.
Full detailOn one PC choose LAN Game, Create, and Deathmatch; set a small nonzero score or time limit and enable any stock map. Join from the second PC, score through kills or the selected dogtag rule, and finish the round in first place.

Win a Team Deathmatch round

Game mode

HintTry the two-team rules and avoid friendly-fire penalties.
WhereHost Team Deathmatch, place players on opposing teams, and reach the team score limit.
Full detailCreate a LAN Team Deathmatch with a finite limit and at least two players. Score against the other team through kills or dogtags as configured, avoid damaging teammates, and let one team reach the victory condition.

Game mode

HintThis mode changes roles across rounds, so experience each side.

Full detailCreate a LAN Assassination match, set Number of Rounds per Player to one or more, and enable exfiltration. As an assassin, eliminate the marked target, collect the dogtag, and reach the extraction zone; as a bodyguard, complete a round by keeping the target alive or eliminating all assassins.

Play every bundled multiplayer map

Game mode
