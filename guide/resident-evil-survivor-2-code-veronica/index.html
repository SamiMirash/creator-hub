Earn S on Every Arcade Stage

Rank

HintAccurate close shots and an unbroken life bar drive the best results.

Score Breakable Scenery

Score Rule

HintOrdinary route objects contribute small but useful points.
WhereDestroy marked crates and safe breakables before each exit.
Full detailShoot every red-cross crate that does not threaten the timer, along with eligible lamps and drums. Each crate adds 200 points, and a full-health first-aid pickup adds a separate bonus, so route these between enemy waves rather than after opening the exit.

Use Critical Hits

Score Rule

HintVery close, centered shots multiply enemy value.
WhereApproach safely and land critical hits on suitable targets.
Full detailStun or isolate a target, step into the short critical range, center the reticle, and fire once. Do not force this against crowded or lunging enemies; preserving the no-damage bonus can be worth more than a risky multiplier.

Preserve the No-Damage Chain

Score Rule

HintClean stages build a growing end-of-stage bonus.
WhereClear consecutive stages without losing health.
Full detailLearn enemy entrances on a practice file, retreat before ambush triggers, and take no damage from Stage 1 onward. A hit breaks the cumulative chain, so restart early if the current run is intended to record all five S grades.

Use Only the Basic Weapon

Score Rule

HintA restricted arsenal earns an end-of-stage bonus.
WhereFinish a stage without firing a collected secondary weapon.
Full detailKeep Claire's handgun or Steve's Golden Lugers equipped for the full stage. A solo basic-weapon clear awards 10,000 points; a two-player clear awards 5,000, so reserve this tactic for routes whose boss can be handled safely without a pickup.

Avoid Continues on Ranking Runs

Score Warning

HintContinuing preserves progress but destroys the active score.
WhereReach the stage result without using Continue.
Full detailIf defeated during an S-grade attempt, restart rather than continuing because the accumulated score returns to zero. Use unlocked continues only for exploration, message collection, or time-based option grinding.
