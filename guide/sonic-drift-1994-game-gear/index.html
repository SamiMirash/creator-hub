Before Starting

You are here when: the cartridge title, main menu, driver selector, road display, and Options screen

Confirm the original handheld route and practise the shared systems before beginning a six-race cup.

Original Game Gear Cartridge

Version check

HintBegin with the preserved handheld build.
WhereUse the Japanese Game Gear cartridge carrying English interface text.
Full detailVerify the extracted 524,288-byte image has SHA-1 FEF5D69093DF2D774ED274A4079790F8778577FC. Do not substitute either promotional demo: this selected product G-3415 cartridge contains the complete retail modes and has no update or add-on content.

Three Play Modes

Mode set

HintThe main menu supports more than cups.
WhereConfirm Chaos GP, Free Run, and Versus appear on the retail menu.
Full detailOpen each menu once without beginning permanent record work. Chaos GP is the four-driver championship, Free Run stores solo times, and Versus needs a genuine or emulated Gear-to-Gear connection between two cartridge instances.

Cup Scoring

Race rule

HintA clean series matters more than one lap.
WhereComplete three laps, then check the 3-2-1-0 placement points.
Full detailEach cup contains six races. First place earns three points, second two, third one, and fourth none. If totals tie after race six, the driver with more race victories ranks higher; only first overall receives that cup's emerald.

Brake Drift

Driving technique

HintTight bends reward a measured second input.
WhereTurn toward a bend while tapping Button 1 to begin sliding.
Full detailBrake before the apex while steering, then release Button 1 and accelerate through the exit. Holding brake and steering together for too long produces a spin, so use brief corrections instead of one continuous slide.

Road Discipline

Driving technique

HintThe painted roadway protects your momentum.
WhereKeep all four wheels on the road and avoid roadside barriers.
Full detailRough shoulders reduce acceleration, while a solid obstacle can stop the vehicle. Enter narrow sections from the centre, brake before the boundary rather than after crossing it, and rebuild speed only once the car points down the next straight.

Two-Ring Special Power

Resource mechanic

HintA small reserve activates each driver's signature move.
WhereCollect at least two rings, then press Up during a race.
Full detailTwo held rings pay for Sonic's Dash or Tails's Jump. A crash or spin removes one ring, so keep a spare pair before a difficult sequence. Eggman and Amy reserve their offensive specials for the two-player mode.

Spring

Track item

HintA yellow road device bypasses low trouble.
WhereDrive over a yellow spring before an obstacle or broken road.
Full detailApproach it squarely rather than from the shoulder. The launch carries the vehicle over nearby opponents, obstacles, or a road gap; steer toward the road centre while airborne so the landing preserves acceleration.

Dash Monitor

Track item

HintThe red box is strongest on open pavement.
WhereBreak a red monitor shortly before a straight section.
Full detailLine up centrally, collect the red box, and let its speed burst run on asphalt. Avoid taking it directly into a sharp corner: braking away the effect or leaving the road wastes both the increased speed and its acceleration benefit.

Invincibility Monitor

Track item

HintThe blue box protects an aggressive route briefly.
WhereBreak a blue monitor before a dense obstacle sequence.
Full detailUse the short protected interval to cross ordinary collision hazards or an opponent's active special without losing speed. It does not correct poor steering, so stay aligned with the roadway instead of treating the effect as permission to drive outside it.

Sonic and the Cyclone

Driver route

HintOne driver trades cornering for rapid pace.
WhereChoose Sonic to combine quick acceleration with a high maximum speed.
Full detailBrake earlier than the handling-focused drivers and use short drifts to protect the Cyclone's line. Hold two rings for Dash, trigger it after the car is straight, and avoid spending the burst immediately before a tight bend.

Tails and the MTP-01 Whirlwind

Driver route

HintThe balanced machine forgives unfamiliar turns.
WhereChoose Tails for steady cornering and moderate speed.
Full detailCarry speed through gentle bends and save two rings for Jump. Activate it before a road hazard or while centrifugal force would otherwise push the car outward; correct toward the centre before landing.

Eggman and the Egg Typhoon

Driver route

HintThe heaviest machine needs room to recover.
WhereChoose Eggman for high maximum speed but slow acceleration.
Full detailProtect momentum by braking before, not inside, each corner. In solo cups the rings are mainly collision insurance because Mine Drop is unavailable there; in Versus, spend two rings where the following driver cannot avoid the mine.

Amy and the Breeze

Driver route

HintStable cornering offsets a lower speed ceiling.
WhereChoose Amy for strong acceleration and predictable handling.
Full detailTake a smooth inside line and rely on quick recovery after unavoidable contact. Heart Toss is unavailable in solo cups, so preserve rings there; in Versus, launch it with a rival ahead and enough road left to capitalize.

Green Chaos GP

You are here when: the green-emblem circuit set, using the gentlest version of each zone

The opening championship teaches the six-zone order with the widest margins.

Green Hill — Green Layout

Cup race

HintUse the early course to establish clean steering before spending items.
WhereRace Green Hill as round 1 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Green Hill layout. Centre the car for the broad turns, avoid the slowing grass, and use a red monitor only after the road opens. A two-ring ability is safer after a bend than while the roadway is still curving. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Marble — Green Layout

Cup race

HintStone boundaries make early braking more valuable than late correction.
WhereRace Marble as round 2 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Marble layout. Approach confined bends from mid-road, tap brake while turning, and release it as soon as the nose faces the exit. A wall impact costs far more time than surrendering a little entry speed. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Spring Yard — Green Layout

Cup race

HintLet the track devices solve hazards without disrupting your line.
WhereRace Spring Yard as round 3 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Spring Yard layout. Meet springs and monitors from the road centre, steer gently during any launch, and land pointing down the following lane. Bank two rings so one spin does not remove immediate access to a special move. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Labyrinth — Green Layout

Cup race

HintNarrow scenery rewards patience and a visible exit line.
WhereRace Labyrinth as round 4 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Labyrinth layout. Slow before the most confined corners and postpone acceleration until the next straight is visible. Use blue protection for crowded hazard sequences, but continue steering normally because boundary contact still destroys momentum. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Star Light — Green Layout

Cup race

HintLonger flowing turns favour controlled speed over repeated braking.
WhereRace Star Light as round 5 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Star Light layout. Set the steering angle early, make one brief brake correction, and unwind toward the centre. Save Dash or a red monitor for the clearer straights, where the higher speed can run without forcing an off-road exit. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Scrap Brain — Green Layout

Cup race

HintThe closing course punishes any hurried approach to machinery.
WhereRace Scrap Brain as round 6 of the Green Chaos GP and finish all three laps.
Full detailThis is the gentlest Scrap Brain layout. Enter mechanical sections centrally, clear road devices squarely, and brake before the scenery narrows. Protect two rings or a blue monitor for the final hazards; restarting momentum here can decide the cup total. First place gives three cup points, but protect the overall lead rather than forcing a collision for one position.

Green Chaos Emerald

Cup reward

HintConsistency across six races earns the cup's reward.
WhereFinish first in the overall Green Chaos GP standings after Scrap Brain.
Full detailAccumulate the highest total under the 3-2-1-0 scoring system. A tie is decided by race-win count. If another driver takes the cup, restart this six-race series; first overall receives the green emerald.

Four Driver Routes

You are here when: the driver selector followed by the complete green, yellow, and red championship sequence

Each machine changes handling and has its own non-story completion presentation.

Sonic Full Route

Driver completion

HintCarry the speed-focused machine through every cup.
WhereWin Green, Yellow, and Red Chaos GP as Sonic.
Full detailBegin a clean Sonic route and take first overall in all three championships. Use Dash on straights, preserve the Cyclone through tight corners, and continue through the red-cup results until Sonic's unique completion presentation and credits finish.

Tails Full Route

Driver completion

HintBalanced handling makes the unfamiliar layouts easier to learn.
WhereWin Green, Yellow, and Red Chaos GP as Tails.
Full detailComplete all eighteen cup races on one Tails route, using Jump to cross disruptive road sections without wasting momentum. Win the red standings and allow Tails's distinct completion presentation and credits to finish.

Eggman Full Route

Driver completion

HintProtect the slow-starting machine's hard-earned momentum.
WhereWin Green, Yellow, and Red Chaos GP as Eggman.
Full detailTake first overall in every cup without relying on Mine Drop, which is disabled in solo racing. Brake early, avoid full stops, and finish the red championship to record Eggman's separate completion presentation and credits.

Amy Full Route

Driver completion

HintA stable machine can win without the highest ceiling.
WhereWin Green, Yellow, and Red Chaos GP as Amy.
Full detailUse Breeze's acceleration and cornering to hold clean inside lines through all eighteen races. Heart Toss is unavailable against the solo field; win through driving and monitor use, then record Amy's unique completion presentation and credits.

Free Run Results Audit

Record audit

HintThe records page proves that every layout was finished.
WhereOpen Results and inspect all eighteen colour-and-zone combinations.
Full detailFor each layout, confirm a three-lap circuit result and a best lap. The table can retain three circuit times plus one fastest lap, but one valid entry per layout is the completion requirement; capture the pages before replacing records.

Free Run Quick Exit

Menu command

HintPractice has a controller-only return command.
WherePause during Free Run, then hold Button 1 and Button 2 together.
Full detailPress Start to pause, hold both action buttons for roughly one second, and wait for the main menu. This shortcut is documented for Free Run; do not assume it aborts Chaos GP without resetting that cup.

Versus Mode

You are here when: two synchronized Game Gear views connected through the cartridge link interface

Treat the link route as real completion work; a grayed menu or disconnected second controller is not a pass.

Gear-to-Gear Connection

Link setup

HintThe multiplayer menu needs two synchronized cartridge instances.
WhereConnect two Game Gears, two Sonic Drift cartridges, and a Gear-to-Gear cable.
Full detailPower both systems off before connecting the cable, insert one matching cartridge in each, then start both and enter Versus. On PC, use an emulator configuration that genuinely emulates the serial link; two local controllers alone do not prove this route.

Complete a Versus Race

Two-player race

HintA second human changes which abilities function.
WhereChoose two different drivers and finish one three-lap Versus race.
Full detailConfirm both instances advance from the same start, complete all laps without a communication timeout, and reach the result screen. Repeat with the opposite host order if the emulator exposes asymmetric link behavior.

Eggman Mine Drop

Versus ability

HintOne offensive special exists only with another player.
WhereCollect two rings as Eggman in Versus, move ahead, and press Up.
Full detailDrop the mine on the racing line after a blind bend or narrow approach. Have the trailing player contact it once, confirm the disruption and ring loss, then repeat with an avoidance line so both the effect and counterplay are documented.

Amy Heart Toss

Versus ability

HintThe other offensive special also requires link play.
WhereCollect two rings as Amy in Versus, line up behind the rival, and press Up.
Full detailLaunch the heart while the other car remains in front and visible, then use Breeze's acceleration to capitalize on the slowdown. Confirm the ability consumes the required rings; it cannot be demonstrated against solo cup opponents.

Options and Difficulty

You are here when: the Options screen containing Sound and the Normal or Hard difficulty selector

Finish one championship under the higher opponent setting and exercise the full built-in audio menu.

Hard Difficulty

Difficulty setting

HintThe second setting strengthens the rival launch pace.
WhereOpen Options and change Difficulty from Normal to Hard.
Full detailConfirm Hard remains selected before returning to Chaos GP. Rival acceleration increases; your own vehicle statistics and the cup layouts do not change, so defend the opening line and avoid any full stop that gives the field a free recovery gap.

Hard Red Chaos GP Win

Difficulty clear

HintUse the hardest setting on the tightest championship.
WhereTake first overall in Red Chaos GP while Difficulty is set to Hard.
Full detailChoose the driver whose handling you control best, verify Hard before entry, and preserve an uninterrupted six-race result. Manage the 3-2-1-0 standings rather than restarting every non-win; first overall after Scrap Brain completes this difficulty route.

Sound Menu Audit

Audio catalogue

HintThe built-in audio list is available immediately.
WhereOpen Options, enter Sound, and play every selectable entry once.
Full detailAdvance through the complete numeric range without skipping a value, start each entry long enough to distinguish music from an effect, and capture the selector at both ends of the range. Stop playback before returning to the main menu.
