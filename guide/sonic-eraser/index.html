Setup and Core Rules

You are here when: The black playfield with a seven-column grid and a falling four-piece cluster.

Use the preserved Mega Drive build through the selected Genesis-compatible emulator route.

Learn the Falling-Cluster Controls

System task

HintUse the opening drops to learn movement before the board becomes crowded.
WhereMove a falling cluster with Left or Right, accelerate it with Down, and rotate it counterclockwise with B or C.
Full detailA cluster contains four linked pieces. Shift it before contact, rotate with B or C to change their order around the cluster, and use Down only after its landing column and orientation are safe.

Trigger a Clear and Collapse

System task

HintArrange matching pieces so gravity can improve the next match.
WherePlace at least two identical pieces edge-to-edge; they vanish and any unsupported pieces above them descend.
Full detailMake one adjacent pair beneath a different matching pair. When the lower pair disappears, the unsupported upper pieces fall; if they touch their match, the board resolves another clear as a chain.

Identify All Seven Ordinary Shapes

Ruleset entry

HintWatch several clusters before assuming the colour set is complete.
WhereConfirm the ordinary set: white square, green square, blue circle, yellow upright triangle, red inverted triangle, orange diamond, and purple cross.
Full detailRecord each of the seven shape-and-colour combinations once. Matching uses identity, not approximate colour; the two triangles point in opposite directions and count as different pieces.

Solo Endless Modes

You are here when: The mode menu listing Normal, Doubt, and Block beside Round Mode.

These score-driven rulesets have no authored final board, so completion records their distinct mechanics and the established Normal benchmark.

Reach Normal Mode Level 10

Mode task

HintBuild steady chains while leaving one recovery column open.
WhereChoose Normal Mode and continue until its level display reaches 10.
Full detailNormal has no finite board list, so Level 10 is the documented completion benchmark. Clear low and wide, reserve one column for awkward clusters, and reduce fast drops as the automatic speed increases.

Trigger Doubt Mode's White Conversion

Mode mechanic

HintExpect a landed cluster to change after it settles.
WhereChoose Doubt Mode and watch a non-white piece turn into a white square on landing.
Full detailWhite squares do not arrive as ordinary falling pieces here. Leave potential white partners accessible, land several clusters until a conversion occurs, then erase the converted square by placing it beside another white square.

Release a Block Mode Tower

Mode mechanic

HintBuild one rigid formation, then look for the match that frees it.
WhereChoose Block Mode, land a cluster so it remains supported as one unit, then create a matching pair beneath it.
Full detailUnlike the ordinary rules, landed clusters can stay rigid instead of separating immediately. Stack a small tower, complete one pair at its support, and confirm that the released formation collapses and resolves any new contacts.

Two-Player Modes

You are here when: The title menu entries labelled 1P AND 2P and 1P VS 2P.

Side-by-side play allows different rulesets on the two fields; direct versus uses the competitive rules.

Play Side-by-Side Round Mode

Two-field task

HintOpen both playfields and verify the mode can run independently on each side.
WhereChoose 1P AND 2P, assign Round Mode, and clear the spiral targets on at least one side.
Full detailActivate the second controller if two players are present; otherwise leave it idle and use the first field. Each side can choose its own ruleset, so confirm the Round behaviour on the active field before leaving the match.

Complete a Human Versus Match

Versus task

HintKeep both fields visible and compare safe stack height before attacking.
WhereChoose 1P VS 2P and play until the ten-minute clock expires or one field fills.
Full detailCompetitive play cannot be paused. Win by holding the higher score at time expiry or by forcing the opposing field to overflow; keep a clean lane available whenever the middle display signals interference.

Send a Mystery Cluster

Versus mechanic

HintBuild a larger simultaneous clear during a versus match.
WhereIn a versus match, erase at least five pieces in one resolution to send a concealed cluster.
Full detailPrepare a connected group of five or more matching pieces, complete the last contact, and watch the opposing preview receive an unidentified cluster whose contents remain hidden until landing.

Trigger Rival-Control Disruption

Versus mechanic

HintReserve a very large clear for the competitive field.
WhereIn a versus match, erase at least seven pieces in one resolution to trigger the stronger attack.
Full detailJoin seven or more identical pieces before completing their contact. The resulting attack briefly interferes with the rival's falling-cluster control, so verify the effect while both fields remain active.
